🔍 Step-by-Step Installation Process

1️⃣ Site Survey & Camera Angle Planning
Inspecting entry points, blind spots, and light conditions to determine optimal camera placement and lens focal length.
2️⃣ Clean Cabling & Hardware Mounting
Routing concealed cables through PVC conduits and mounting cameras securely on walls, ceilings, or outdoor poles.
3️⃣ DVR/NVR & Network Configuration
Connecting storage drives, configuring recording schedules, setting up motion detection alerts, and assigning static IP/P2P cloud settings.
4️⃣ Mobile App Sync & Client Training
Pairing the security system with your mobile phone, testing night vision quality, and training your team on viewing historical playback footage.
